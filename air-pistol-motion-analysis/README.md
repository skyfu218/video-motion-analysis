# Air Pistol Motion Analysis

Air Pistol Motion Analysis is a local-first video review tool for visualizing aiming stability, selected-region movement, posture, and synchronized single-camera 3D motion estimates.

![Air Pistol Motion Analysis interface](../assets/air-pistol/air-pistol-interface.png)

## What it demonstrates

- Camera capture or local video import
- User-selected ROI for movement tracking
- X-Y movement trace and stability indicators
- Side-view posture and joint-angle visualization
- Synchronized single-camera 3D pose review
- Manual analysis-range correction and frame controls
- Coach-facing summary, CSV data, and PNG report workflow

## Project links

- [Live demo](https://air-pistol-3d-skyfu218.web.app/)

## Measurement and safety boundary

ROI movement, depth, joint angles, and 3D views are camera-based estimates affected by perspective, occlusion, image texture, stabilization, and model confidence. The application does not determine shooting skill, predict score, replace a calibrated aiming-trace system, or provide medical or firearm-safety decisions. Shooting activity must follow range rules, with the device operated from a safe position.
